# Jansen-Rit 1995 preregistration P3 v1 (draft, UNSIGNED: no scored run may use it)

Paper Fig. 3 (single column, C sweep under uniform random input), facts from `../SOURCE_P1.md`. z is the jaxfne
emitter `simulate_jansen_rit` at the standard set (no fit). The paper gives no step size, noise hold or run length,
so the estimands score regime class, dominant frequency and EEG level, not waveform.

## Fixed protocol

Driver `run_jr_fig3.py --seeds S --out <dir>` at its defaults: C in {68, 128, 135, 270, 675, 1350}; A 3.25 mV,
B 22 mV, a 100/s, b 50/s, e0 2.5/s, v0 6 mV, r 0.56/mV, C1..C4 = C, 0.8C, 0.25C, 0.25C; p uniform 120-320
pulses/s, one draw held per 2 ms (assumed: Fig. 3's sample interval, 500 units = 1 s); fixed-step RK4,
dt 0.1 ms (paper: RKF45); 10 s from the zero state, first 1 s discarded; float64 on CPU. Spectrum: Hann-windowed
4 s segments, 50% overlap, peak in 1-30 Hz; oscillation index `osc` = band power within ±1 Hz of the peak over
1-30 Hz band power. One frozen clone; its SHA goes in the result.

## Estimands

Classes, fixed here: noise if `osc` < 0.4; alpha if `osc` ≥ 0.4 and f_peak in 8-13 Hz; spike-wave if `osc` ≥ 0.4,
f_peak < 8 Hz and EEG SD ≥ 5 mV.

| Id | y (paper, p. 360-361) | z | criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 sequence | 68 noise; 128 noisy alpha; 135 alpha; 270, 675 spike-wave-like; 1350 noise-like | class per C | classes noise, alpha, alpha, spike-wave, spike-wave, noise |
| E2 alpha sharpens | noisy alpha at 128, well-defined at 135 | `osc` at 128 and 135 | `osc`(135) > `osc`(128) |
| E3 slowing | spike-wave frequency falls with C | f_peak at 270 and 675 | f(675) < f(270) |
| E4 level | output between 5 and 10 at the standard set (p. 361, units mV, inferred) | mean EEG at C = 135 | 5 ≤ mean ≤ 10 mV |
