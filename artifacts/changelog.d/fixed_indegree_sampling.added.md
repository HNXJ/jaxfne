- Connection rules gain `sampling="fixed_indegree"` with `in_degree=K`: every post cell draws exactly `K` distinct presynaptic partners uniformly without replacement. Declared on `Configuration.connections()`, `InterConnection`/`AreaConnection` (forwarded by JDNA `develop()`); refused with `probability`, `max_in_degree`, `footprint` and the other samplings, on the conductance (adex) route, and on the sign-only fallback path. A post cell with fewer than `K` available partners raises instead of filling short.
