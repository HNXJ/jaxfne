- `spectrolaminar_psd_jax` forms each DFT phase as `(f * n mod fs) / fs` from exact float32 remainders, so long recordings keep float32 precision under `jit`. The phase `(f / fs) * n` lost about log2(n) bits: on broadband noise the PSD missed a float64 DFT at the same frequencies by 2e-4 of its peak at 10 s and 2e-2 at 1000 s (fs 1 kHz); it now stays near 1e-6. Every PSD changes at rounding level.
