- Registered HDP rule `ephaptic_field` (`jaxfne.hdp_rules_ephaptic`): endogenous ephaptic coupling \(\Phi_i=\alpha\sum_j M_{ij}(v_j-v_{\mathrm{rest}})\) on the Izhikevich and LIF routes, entering the membrane through `drive_bias` at the next step (from post-step voltages on LIF, pre-step voltages on Izhikevich). `ephaptic_kernel` builds a sparse Gaussian kernel \(M\) from positions (cutoff, optional row normalization); cost scales with kernel pairs, never \(N^2\). \(\alpha=0\) reproduces the uncoupled run; without the rule nothing changes.
