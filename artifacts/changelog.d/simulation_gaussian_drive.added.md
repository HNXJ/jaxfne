- `Simulation(gaussian_drive={"std_by_cell_type": {...}})` sets each cell's stochastic-current coefficient on the Izhikevich routes (the kernels' `noise_scale` at the 0.5 ms reference step, scaled by `sqrt(0.5/dt)`); the mapping names the model's cell types, and other emitter families refuse the declaration by name, as do runs that also set `noise_scale` in `hdp_params` or `homeostasis_params`.
