- The `VIP` and `I` Izhikevich presets integrate Izhikevich 2004's class-1 v equation, `0.04 v^2 + 4.1 v + 108 - u + I`, which their `a`/`b`/`c`/`d` (figure1.m panel G) belong to; they previously ran in the standard `5 v + 140` equation. Rheobase falls from 22.56 to 2.25, so an uncoupled `VIP` or `I` cell fires at its default drive 3.0. Every realization with a `VIP` or `I` cell changes; all-standard populations are unchanged. `IzhikevichParams` gains `v_lin` and `v_const` (per cell; `None` is the standard 5 and 140), carried through `connect()` and checkpoints. A checkpoint written before this change restores with the standard equation it was run with.
