- `Configuration.connections()` gains `intra_step_rounds=R` on the AdEx route: a declared positive-int round cap admitting a `delay_ms` that rounds to 0 steps on that connection only. Within one step, round 0 is the existing step, then rounds propagate the delay-0 conductance of newly spiked cells to their targets and re-step every cell not yet spiked from the pre-step state, until no newly spiked cell owns a delay-0 out-edge; spikes are kept once fired. A step that reaches the cap with undelivered events refuses the run, naming the step. `simulate_adex` returns `fixed_point_rounds` and `fixed_point_unresolved` per step; the run manifest records `intra_step_fixed_point` (`rounds` cap, `max_rounds_used`, `n_unresolved_spikes`, `n_delay0_edges`, `rule`) when delay-0 edges exist; runs without them take the unchanged scan body. Refused: a non-positive-int R, R on a connection whose delay reaches 1 step or more, R on other routes, and a 0-step delay without R.
