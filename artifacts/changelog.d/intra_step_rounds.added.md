- `Configuration.connections()` gains `intra_step_rounds=R` on the AdEx route: a declared positive-int round count admitting a `delay_ms` that rounds to 0 steps on that connection only. Within one step, round 0 is the existing step, then R rounds propagate the delay-0 conductance of newly spiked cells to their targets and re-step every cell not yet spiked from the pre-step state; spikes are kept once fired. The run manifest records `intra_step_fixed_point` (`rounds`, `n_delay0_edges`, `rule`) when delay-0 edges exist; runs without them take the unchanged scan body. Refused: a non-positive-int R, R on a connection whose delay reaches 1 step or more, R on other routes, and a 0-step delay without R.
