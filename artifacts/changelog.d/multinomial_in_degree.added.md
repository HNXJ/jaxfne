- `in_degree=k` with `sampling="multinomial"` draws `round(k*n_post)` synapses per rule, drawn with replacement, so `k` is the mean in-degree: a finite number > 0, non-integer allowed, on `Configuration.connections`, `AreaConnection` and `InterConnection`. `in_degree` together with `probability` is refused. The compiled connection row records the declared `in_degree` and the realized count `realized_k`. Probability-form multinomial rules draw the same edges as before.
