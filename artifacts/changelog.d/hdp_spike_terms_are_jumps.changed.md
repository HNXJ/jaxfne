- The HDP probe rules `synthetic_presyn_gain` and `eligibility_trace_gain` now apply their spike terms as per-event jumps: `H` gains `k_h` once per presynaptic arrival, and the eligibility and edge-weight spike terms are jumps too. The Izhikevich, LIF and AdEx registered kernels integrate a jump once, with the `dt_ms` factor removed. Their outputs change at every `dt_ms` other than 1; at `dt_ms=1` they agree with the earlier values up to rounding.
- `HDPRuleUpdate` gains optional jump fields `jH`, `j_aux` and `j_theta` (default `None`). A rule leaving the jumps at their `None` default integrates as before. Superposed rules carry jumps through.
