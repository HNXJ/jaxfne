- `fields.kernel_lfp`: hybrid kernel LFP (Hagen et al. 2016). Vertical passive cable cells (`cable_cell`, and `profile_cell` from depth profiles of membrane area and axial-equivalent diameter, with a per-compartment `area_factor`), area-weighted synapse placement by depth, exact passive cable response and transmembrane currents, line-source potentials averaged over lateral cell distances, per-synapse laminar kernels in mV, and their convolution with network spike counts. Every biophysical value is a required argument; invalid values are refused.
- `fields.hagen2016_cells`: the Hagen et al. (2016) hybrid-LFP morphologies reduced to 10 µm depth profiles (SHA-256 of each source file recorded), and `hagen2016_cell(population, target_bounds_um)` placing a population's cell in a column with given layer boundaries.
