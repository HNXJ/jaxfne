- The current-route Poisson drive gains the optional key `counts=C` (default `1`): each step per cell sums `C` independent trains, drawn as one `binomial(C, p)` per step per cell. `counts=1` keeps the historical Bernoulli drive bit for bit; non-positive-int values are refused. Declared on `Simulation(poisson_drive=...)` and carried on the `XSource` current-route schedule (recorded only when declared).
