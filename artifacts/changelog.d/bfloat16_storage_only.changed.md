- `bfloat16` is a storage dtype only. Every route integrates its dynamics in float32 and casts its recorded arrays (voltages, sources, field readouts, seed-batch outputs) to bfloat16 once at the end; the time axis is float32. Before, the homeostatic EI and AdEx seed-batch routes integrated in bfloat16, the edge-list Izhikevich route drew its noise in bfloat16, and the LIF route rounded its drive to bfloat16. `RuntimeConfig` gains `compute_dtype` and `jnp_compute_dtype`; `actual_dtype` still reports `bfloat16`.
