- `Simulation(poisson_drive=[...])` runs on the adex route: one mapping per entry over pairwise disjoint cell-type targets, each entry carrying its mechanism, rate and seed. A list of one matches the mapping bit for bit at nonzero rate (and records the mapping form). Each entry draws under its own seed stream, so one entry's seed leaves every other entry's spikes unchanged. A zero-rate entry in a list draws no counts and silences its targets; the mapping form still refuses `rate_hz <= 0`.
