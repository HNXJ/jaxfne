jaxfne replication lanes

One card per lane folder in artifacts/etudes/papers, read from its README, its files, git, the replication registry and the todo and open stacks.

Title and caption come from the lane README (title line, first sentence of the first paragraph), or from SOURCE_P1.md (its Target paragraph) or NOTES.md where the lane has no README. Receipts are the lane's score files (score*.json, scores/*.json) and any json named receipt. Standing is the registry stage and class from dashboard/data/replications.json, joined to the folder by the rule in scripts/check_replications.py. The render date is the git commit date of the newest PNG in the lane, or of its dashboard/figures render; an untracked file shows its file time and says so. A render date shows when the picture was committed, not whether its numbers were reviewed. A receipt count is not freshness: jaxfne has no per-receipt staleness judge, and the registry document's own judge (scripts/build_dashboard_data.py --check) rates the whole document only. Open rows are todo table rows, T- bullets and O- lines that name the lane folder, a registry id, or the author and year.
